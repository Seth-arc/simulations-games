The learning logic

Practice the judgment.
Reconstruct the decision.

A simulation becomes a learning intervention when participants can connect what happened with how they reasoned.

I connect actor roles and constraints with evidence, decisions and consequences, then use facilitated review to examine the alternatives. The design makes uncertainty visible and keeps the boundaries of the model explicit.
